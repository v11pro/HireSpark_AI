About AI-Powered Recruitment & Talent Marketplace

The AI-Powered Recruitment & Talent Marketplace is an AI-powered platform designed to automate resume screening and candidate ranking. It uses GenAI to understand job descriptions and resumes, extract relevant skills and experience, and provide intelligent candidate-matching insights.

Traditional TF-IDF and KNN techniques are combined with GenAI-based semantic analysis to improve resume matching, reduce recruiter effort, save time, and identify the most suitable candidates efficiently.
